## Decision (recorded 2026-08-10)

**Beside, not under.** vfs and agentfs are siblings over one graph store + blob store; neither consumes the other. AgentFS (`rustyred-thg-agentfs`) is "the filesystem is a view; the graph is the truth": ino-keyed POSIX (inode nodes, `CHILD` edges, 64KB chunk refs into the blob layer; `readdir`/`lookup`/`getattr`/`read`/`write`/`mkdir`/...; `fsck` oracle; AF2 typed edges; AF3 branch/overlay; `fuse_host` as a feature-gated integration layer above the graph API). VFS (`rustyred-thg-vfs`) is an IntelliJ-semantics journaling mirror of the REAL filesystem: `mount`/`refresh`/`snapshot`/`subscribe` with generation pointers, content hashes + blob-backed bytes — the real FS is an input, the graph journal is the durable record. For IDE workspaces: **graph-native workspaces serve through agentfs** (the theorem-proxy `AgentFs` impl calls `AgentFsHost` — `lookup`+`read_at` for NewBuffer, `readdir`+`getattr` for ReadDir, rev checks at the proxy layer for Save); **host directory workspaces serve through vfs's journal** (generation cursors are the store-subscription watcher seam) with the stock `HostFs` passthrough; `fuse_host` (agentfs) is the LSP/PTY/git mountpoint when AgentFs-backed, mirror-to-directory the no-FUSE fallback. Consequence for theorem-proxy: the `WorkspaceBackend` classifies the workspace at open and selects HostFs vs AgentFs accordingly; the handoff mapping table maps one-to-one onto `AgentFsHost`'s POSIX surface, so no vfs-under-agentfs composition is needed. Bonus finding recorded: the absorption's VFS lane already exists in-substrate as `rustyred-thg-vfs` ("mined from the IntelliJ Platform's snapshot/refresh/typed-event semantics") — feeds p-l3's K-lane mapping.
